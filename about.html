About Kikoma Global Investment Limited

West Africa's Premier Gateway for Agricultural Commodities & Cross-Border Freight Operations

Corporate Profile

Kikoma Global Investment Limited is an accredited Nigerian corporate enterprise headquartered in Idiroko, Ipokia Local Government Area, Ogun State. We specialize in international agricultural commodity sourcing, processing quality control, export documentation, and customs clearance logistics.

Positioned along the strategic trade corridor of Nigeria’s western border, our export operations connect global industries in Europe, Asia, and America with Grade-1 West African harvests.

Regulatory Compliance

SCUML Verification: SCUML Compliant

Chamber Membership: NACCIMA & Yewa Chamber of Commerce & Industry

Core Values

Quality Assurance: Strict laboratory testing and slate analysis for all commodity batches.

Weight Precision: Digital scale calibration for every single jute bag prior to export.

Reliability: Seamless export documentation and Form NXP compliance.
