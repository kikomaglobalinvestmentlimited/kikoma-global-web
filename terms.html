Terms of Trade & Export Conditions

Governing policies, weighing protocols, and warehousing standards for Kikoma Global Investment Limited.

1. Scope & Application

These Terms of Trade govern all commodity storage, bulk bagging, digital scale certification, and cross-border export logistics services provided by Kikoma Global Investment Limited at our Idiroko storage complex.

2. Commodity Quality & Packaging

All agricultural produce and solid mineral aggregates processed or stored within our facilities must meet Grade-1 export standards. Commodities are packaged in secure, brand-tagged jute bags or heavy-duty woven sacks under strict moisture and pest-controlled environmental protocols.

3. Certified Weighing & Inspection

Weight verification is conducted using calibrated heavy-duty industrial trade scales at our Idiroko hub. Certified pre-shipment inspection reports are issued prior to container stuffing and customs dispatch.

4. Liability & Storage Conditions

While Kikoma Global maintains 24/7 facility security and environmental monitoring, clients are advised to secure comprehensive cargo and transit insurance for international shipping routes.
