About Kikoma Global Investment Limited

CAC RC: 8918006 — Built on integrity, institutional discipline, and sustainable impact

Kikoma Global Investment Limited is a premier investment and asset management firm combining rigorous financial oversight with deep operational roots in key growth sectors across West Africa and global trade corridors.

Our mandate is to generate compounding, long-term value for our institutional partners and private investors while championing environmental stewardship and community empowerment.
