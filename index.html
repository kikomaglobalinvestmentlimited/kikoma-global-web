Corporate Overview

Excellence in Agricultural Commodities & Border Logistics Operations

Kikoma Global Investment Limited is a dynamic Nigerian corporate enterprise headquartered in Idiroko, Ipokia Local Government Area, Ogun State. We specialize in international agricultural commodity sourcing, processing quality control, export documentation, and customs clearance logistics.

Positioned at the strategic trade corridor of Nigeria’s western border, our export arm connects European, Asian, and American industrial buyers with ethically sourced, top-tier West African cocoa. Through strict adherence to Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) Form NXP guidelines, international Quality Certificates of Analysis, and transparent trade terms, we ensure seamless global deliveries.
