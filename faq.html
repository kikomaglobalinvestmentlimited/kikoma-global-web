Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear answers regarding our Idiroko storage facilities, digital scale weighing, export procedures, and commercial terms.

Where is the Kikoma Global warehouse facility located?
Our primary high-capacity storage and logistics complex is strategically positioned at Idiroko, Ogun State, providing optimal proximity for cross-border trade, commodity aggregation, and rapid transport dispatch.
How do you guarantee weight accuracy for export commodities?
Every batch of agricultural produce or solid mineral aggregates passing through our hub is meticulously processed and measured using certified heavy-duty digital trade scales prior to container loading, eliminating weight discrepancies.
What packaging standards are used for export goods?
We utilize secure, brand-tagged export-grade jute bags for agricultural commodities and heavy-duty woven sacks for solid minerals, maintained under strict moisture-controlled warehouse conditions.
How can I track my shipment or consignment status?
You can use our live online tracking portal (`track.html`) by entering your unique Consignment Tracking ID (e.g., `KG-IDIROKO-2026`) to view real-time milestone progress from bagging to port dispatch.
How do I contact the commercial sales or executive team?
You can reach our departments directly via email at `Sales@kikomaglobal.com`, `ceo@kikomaglobal.com`, or `exportlead@kikomaglobal.com`, or chat instantly via WhatsApp at +234 913 316 1564.
