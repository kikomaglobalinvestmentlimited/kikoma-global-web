Got Questions? We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about investing with Kikoma Global Investment Limited

What is Kikoma Global Investment Limited?
Kikoma Global Investment Limited is a premier investment and asset management firm focused on high-potential, resilient sectors including agro-allied value chains, prime real estate development, and international trade logistics.
How can I track my active investments?
You can monitor your portfolio performance and milestones in real time by navigating to our Tracking Portal and entering your unique Investment Reference ID or registered email address.
What are the typical investment tenures?
Tenures vary depending on the asset class or product selected. Short-term trade finance pools typically range from 6 to 18 months, while agro-allied funds and real estate notes range from 12 to 36+ months.
How do I get started with an investment?
You can browse our available options in our Catalogue or Products pages, then reach out to our team directly via our Contact Page or official WhatsApp channel (+234 9133161564).
Are my investments secure?
Yes. We deploy rigorous multi-layered risk assessment frameworks, back our instruments with tangible corporate assets, and maintain strict regulatory compliance across all our operational desks.
