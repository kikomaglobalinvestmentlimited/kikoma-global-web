Full Corporate Offer (FCO)

Official trade mandates, commodity specifications, and standardized transaction execution procedures.

Corporate Trade Mandate

Issued by Kikoma Global Investment Limited — Idiroko, Ogun State, Nigeria

1. Overview & Purpose

This Full Corporate Offer (FCO) outlines the binding commercial terms, specification standards, and procedural protocols for prospective buyers and institutional partners engaging with Kikoma Global Investment Limited for agro-allied exports and trade financing.

2. Core Sectors & Commodities

We facilitate direct aggregation, processing, and cross-border distribution across primary agricultural value chains and logistics corridors, ensuring verified quality standards and regulatory compliance.

3. Standard Transaction Procedures (SOP)

  • Step 1: Buyer issues official Letter of Intent (LOI) / Corporate Purchase Order (CPO).
  • Responsible Desk: exportlead@kikomaglobal.com
  • Step 2: Kikoma Global issues Full Corporate Offer (FCO) and Draft Sales & Purchase Agreement (SPA).
  • Step 3: Execution of SPA by both parties with required financial instrument or escrow alignment.
  • Step 4: Commencement of loading, quality inspection (SGS or equivalent), and export dispatch.

4. Official Communication Channels

All official trade offers, LOI submissions, and inquiries must be directed exclusively through our corporate channels:

  • Export & Logistics Lead Desk: exportlead@kikomaglobal.com
  • Sales & Partnerships Desk: sales@kikomaglobal.com
  • Official WhatsApp Line: +234 9133161564
